01 / PROJECT CONTEXT

Preparing data for reliable analysis

For this demo, I worked with 1,000 synthetic e-commerce order records. The raw file contains missing values, exact duplicates, possible duplicate orders, inconsistent date and number formats, and variations in text. I kept the original CSV unchanged, cleaned a separate copy, and recorded the changes.

All records were created for this demonstration; no real customer or transaction data is included.

02 / CLEANING WORKFLOW

How I cleaned and validated the data

  1. 01
    Profile the raw data

    I started with 1,000 rows and 14 columns, checking missing values, duplicates, possible duplicate orders, inconsistent formats, value consistency, and product details.

  2. 02
    Set the rules before cleaning

    I kept the raw CSV unchanged, removed exact duplicates only, and flagged possible duplicates while retaining them. I standardized values only when a verified mapping was available, filled prices only when the synthetic catalog confirmed them, and left other unsupported values blank.

  3. 03
    Apply the rules

    I removed 25 extra exact duplicates, flagged 48 possible duplicates, standardized labels and formats, and filled 8 missing prices using the catalog.

  4. 04
    Validate the result

    The cleaned copy has 975 rows and 15 columns. It has no remaining exact duplicates or inconsistent product details; 50 unsupported missing values across six fields remain documented.

04 / FINDINGS AND LIMITATIONS

What the analysis shows and what to keep in mind

Key findings

  • Net sales totaled SAR 105,895.90 across 539 calculable order lines.
  • March had the highest monthly sales at SAR 12,473.60; July had the lowest at SAR 4,843.00.
  • ArcBeat Earbuds led product sales at SAR 17,157.15. Luma Desk Lamp had the most known units sold: 138.
  • Home & Kitchen led category sales at SAR 47,767.90. Northern was the top assigned region at SAR 26,217.70.

Limitations

  • The dataset is synthetic, so these results demonstrate the workflow and do not represent real business performance. The analysis includes paid and delivered lines and excludes 48 possible-duplicate candidates.
  • Nine eligible lines could not be included in sales totals because quantity or discount was missing. Five lines with missing dates were omitted from the monthly chart.
  • Six lines with missing regions are grouped as “Unassigned.” Net sales are not profit; taxes, shipping, and costs are not included. The data covers 2025 only.
